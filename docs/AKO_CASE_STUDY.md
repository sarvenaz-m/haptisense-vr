## Reconstructed engineering story

The current demonstrator follows a startup-style systems workflow:

1. Define the human interaction and safety problem.
2. Build a device-independent scientific model.
3. Add measurable outputs and deterministic tests.
4. Separate software, device transport, and experimental records.
5. Make limitations and next validation steps explicit.

This is a defensible connection to AKO because it describes transferred
engineering practice rather than inventing historical repository provenance.

## Historical evidence checklist

Before claiming specific AKO haptics/VR achievements, collect whichever items
genuinely exist:

- dated photographs of prototypes or devices;
- screenshots of VR/AR/3D scenes;
- code excerpts or version history with confidential details removed;
- design documents, protocols, reports, presentations, or competition entries;
- product or project names and dates;
- device models and the applicant's exact responsibility;
- anonymized study forms or ethics material where legally shareable;
- a signed reference letter or contactable collaborator.

Do not fabricate or reconstruct historical artefacts. A reference who can
confirm the work is more valuable than a polished but unverifiable claim.
