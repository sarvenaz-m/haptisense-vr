# Background and project scope

## Project status

HaptiSense VR is a new, independent 2026 portfolio research prototype. It was
not developed during employment at AKO Smart Technologies Group and is not an
AKO, university, or clinical deliverable.

The repository demonstrates current software implementation in haptic
modelling, Unity–Python integration, safety gating, deterministic simulation,
and psychophysics tooling. Its committed results contain simulation and
synthetic-observer data only.

## Professional background

Earlier work at AKO provides relevant systems context in:

- smart-healthcare concepts;
- application and web development;
- electronics–software collaboration;
- wearable-sensing concepts;
- practical prototyping in a multidisciplinary R&D setting.

HaptiSense VR applies those transferable systems-engineering skills to a new
haptics problem. It does not imply that the present source code, Unity
integration, Phantom/Geomagic work, surgical simulation, or participant
experiments existed at AKO.

## Academic research background

EEG analysis, biomedical signal processing, and formal research methods belong
to the author's academic background and are separate from the AKO experience.
This repository does not claim that the author acquired EEG from participants;
any statement about the original EEG study should identify separately whether
it used newly acquired recordings or an existing dataset.

## Current evidence boundary

| Demonstrated here | Requires future laboratory evidence |
|---|---|
| Deterministic contact and inertial-cue models | Calibrated force output on a physical device |
| Force–vibration synthesis and safety limits | Latency, jitter, comfort, and hardware safety measurements |
| Import-ready Unity C# integration source | A recorded, runnable Unity scene and device SDK integration |
| Synthetic 2AFC staircase and structured trial logging | Ethics approval, participant recruitment, and human results |

Historical employment evidence and academic-study details should be documented
through their original records. They are intentionally not reconstructed in
this repository.
