# Provenance and contribution scope

HaptiSense VR is an independent portfolio prototype by Sarvenaz Mahmoudzadeh
Khameneh. It is not an official HIITS deliverable or a jury-approved system.

- Public v0.2.1 baseline: `13d65e03220be16da0c27b022c58749cc82272d4`.
- The September bench/firmware additions were previously local review material.
- The v0.3 contact model, browser studio, identification utility, tests and
  documentation were developed with AI assistance during September 2026.
- AI assistance includes implementation and test authoring. The candidate must
  personally review, execute and understand the work before describing personal
  implementation proficiency or presenting it in an interview.
- Recorded tests distinguish Python/JavaScript and host C++ execution from
  unexecuted Unity source and unflashed firmware.
- Browser screenshots are captures of the running software. They are not
  photographs of equipment or physical experiments.
- Model traces and observer responses are computational/synthetic. There is no
  participant study, clinical validation, measured actuator output or certified
  device safety claim.
- The original application records and jury correspondence are not part of this
  public source repository.

MIT licensing applies to the project source. No third-party graphical assets
are needed by the new workbench; geometry and charts are generated in code.
